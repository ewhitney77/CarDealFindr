How the score is built

Weighted average of five 0–100 components (weights: price 45 · mileage 25 · dom 10 · price_drop 10 · distance 10). price: 50 at market, 100 at 15% below, 0 at 15% above; basis is CarGurus IMV when known, else the median of peers in this run. mileage: vs 12,000/yr for the car's age, +15 under 25,000 mi. days-on-market: 20 when brand new on the lot, 100 at 60+ days. price-drop: 40 if never cut, 60+ once cut. distance: 100 at home, 0 at 200 mi. Everything is in config.py; the numbers behind every row are in the SQLite scores table.
